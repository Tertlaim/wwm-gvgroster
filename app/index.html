Quick Guide

  • Register: enter your in-game name and class, then pick Saturday, Sunday, or both. Each day has its own groups and reserves list. Click Click to Register to join. You can register for both days separately.
  • Drag & drop player badges between groups and reserves (mods+).
  • Right-click any player for quick actions: copy to reserve, move to a group, edit, or delete.
  • Keyboard (mods+): click a player to select it, then C copy, M move, E edit, Delete delete; Esc deselects.
  • Bulk actions: tick the checkbox on cards/badges (far right) to select, then use the header buttons.
  • Edit: the icon (far left) opens inline editing; Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  • Password: click the key icon next to your name (top-right, after login) to change your password. New passwords must be at least 4 characters.
  • Groups: use Group Management in Admin Tools to add/remove groups; the trash icon on a group card removes it.
  • Collapse panels: click any panel header to expand/collapse it and save screen space.
  • Live sync: moderator changes reach everyone automatically. While you are typing or dragging, incoming updates wait until you finish, so your work is never wiped.
  • Roles: SuperAdmin () manages admins & mods · Admin () manages mods · Moderator () edits the roster.
  • Export: download the roster as a shareable Image or PDF (tables, ready to send).
  • Export (Admin): export/import the Guild Member list as CSV and download the JSON backup from Admin Tools.

Bot setup guide

One bot per guild — the OAuth2 app on GameVox and the config in this panel are two halves of one connection. Removing either half breaks delivery.

  1. Create the application. Open developers.gamevox.com and sign in with your GameVox account (the same one you use in the app). Scroll to Your applications → + New application. The bot token (GVB.…) is created here and shown only once — copy and keep it for step 5.
  2. Open the OAuth2 tab. In your application, open the OAuth2 tab → URL generator. The bot and applications.commands scopes are ticked by default — leave them exactly as they are.
  3. Pick permissions. Still on the OAuth2 tab, tick in the permissions grid: View Channels, Send Messages, Embed Links, Read Message History (“Administrator” also works but grants far more than needed).
  4. Install the bot. Scroll to the bottom of the OAuth2 tab to reach the link form. Either pick your server and press Install Application, or press Copy on the Generated URL and open it in a browser to install. Do this after the permissions in step 3 are ticked, so the bot installs with them.
  5. Connect it here. Use the bot token from step 1: paste it into BOT TOKEN and press Enter. Right-click each target channel in GameVox → Copy Channel ID, paste into CHANNELS (space/comma separated) and press Enter. Pick the ON PUBLISH behaviour: a fresh message per publish keeps rosters visible at the bottom of chat; edit-mode rewrites one message (pair it with pinning). Publish then delivers instantly, like normal chat.
