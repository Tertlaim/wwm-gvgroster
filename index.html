Quick Guide

  • Register: fill in your in-game name/class/days and click Click to Register.
  • Drag & drop player badges between groups and reserves (mods+).
  • Right-click any player for quick actions: copy to reserve, move to a group, edit, or delete.
  • Keyboard (mods+): click a player to select it, then C copy, M move, E edit, Delete delete; Esc deselects.
  • Bulk actions: tick the checkbox on cards/badges (far right) to select, then use the header buttons.
  • Edit: the icon (far left) opens inline editing; Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  • Password: click the key icon next to your name (top-right, after login) to change your password. New passwords must be at least 4 characters.
  • Groups: use Group Management in Admin Tools to add/remove groups; the trash icon on a group card removes it.
  • Collapse panels: click any panel header to expand/collapse it and save screen space.
  • Live sync: moderator changes reach everyone automatically. While you are typing or dragging, incoming updates wait until you finish, so your work is never wiped.
  • Roles: SuperAdmin () manages admins & mods · Admin () manages mods · Moderator () edits the roster.
  • Export: anyone can download the roster as a shareable Image or PDF (tables, ready to send). Admins can also export/import the Guild Member list as CSV and download the JSON backup from Admin Tools.
